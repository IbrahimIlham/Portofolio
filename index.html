01 Thesis · Machine Learning

Customer Churn Prediction using XGBoost & SHAP

Built an end-to-end churn prediction pipeline on 48,140 e-commerce customers (43.86% churn rate) using the CRISP-DM methodology. Achieved 0.929 Recall and 0.979 ROC-AUC with XGBoost, selected after rigorous benchmarking against Random Forest. Applied SHAP (TreeExplainer) for model explainability — identifying customer duration, purchase frequency, and account lifespan as the dominant drivers of customer churn.
